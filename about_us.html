Our Story At BDG

For over 25 years, our Perrysburg shop has been more than just a place to get custom gear, it’s been a cozy hub where community, craft, and local pride come together under one roof.

Whether you’re gearing up to cheer on Perrysburg, Lake, Genoa, Maumee, Rossford, BG, Northwood, Danbury, or Eastwood, or you’re a local business owner looking to share your story through custom merchandise, we’re here to make you feel right at home. What makes us special isn't just the work we do; it's the heart we put into every order. From vibrant screen printing and heat transfers to detailed embroidery, custom cups, warm blankets, and stadium seats, we take immense pride in crafting pieces that last. While you can always request a custom quote or call us, nothing beats stopping by in person! We love welcoming friendly faces into our store to chat through designs, touch and feel product samples, and pick out ready-to-wear school spirit gear. Step in, say hello, and let’s bring your ideas to life together!
